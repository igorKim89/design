The story

Business tools are usually powerful and tiring. Tixxe makes the daily flow of tasks feel light: clear screens, calm colour and interactions that stay out of the way.

01

Challenge

Task apps for business tend to be heavy: too many screens, too much noise. People open them because they have to, not because they want to.

02

Approach

A clean interface built around one question per screen. Clear hierarchy, a consistent component system and a visual language that works on both iOS and Android.

03

Outcome

A mobile app that feels fast and friendly. Teams plan, track and finish work in fewer taps, and the product looks like one family on every screen.
